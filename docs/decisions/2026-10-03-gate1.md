# Gate 1 decision, 3 Oct 2026

**Verdict: GO on the pre-registered bar, with two caveats that change Milestone 3.**

Source: `results/audit/summary.json`, regenerated by `python -m chhaya.data.audit`.

## The bar

At least 60 Shanghai recordings with 3 or more days of CGM and 2 or more logged meals a day.
Result: **106 usable of 109**. GO.

## Caveat 1: the clean Shanghai cohort is 44 recordings, not 106

Excluding the 65 recordings on insulin (the twin has no exogenous-insulin input yet) leaves **44 usable
recordings, below the bar of 60**. Only 26 of those also have a fasting-insulin value in their record.

Decision: Milestone 3 item 3.7 (insulin as a model input: basal, bolus, pump) moves from "drug terms" to a
first-class task, scheduled right after Gate 2. Until it lands, Shanghai results are reported on the
44-recording non-insulin cohort and labelled as such. Shanghai meals also need the food-to-macros table
(item 3.1) before the twin can use them, so the first reveal runs on CGMacros alone.

## Caveat 2: CGMacros lows are mostly sensor artefacts, so Night Watch cannot stand on it

| Group | Low events | Nights with a low | Time below 70 mg/dL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthy (15) | 364 | 65 | 22.8 % |
| Pre-diabetes (16) | 126 | 23 | 8.2 % |
| T2D (14) | 34 | 7 | 1.5 % |

Healthy participants spend 22.8 % of the time below 70 mg/dL, which is the signature of Libre compression
and over-reading, not physiology. Night Watch would be trained on artefacts. The honest real-event pool for
overnight lows is Shanghai: **59 nights with a low among non-insulin recordings** (99 nights overall).
The fallback pitch stays alive, but only on Shanghai and only with the sustained-15-minutes rule.

## Loader findings on real data (all fixed, tests added)

- CGMacros headers differ from the data dictionary: `METs` not `Mets`; 11 files carry `Intensity` instead
  of METs and one carries `Steps`; 2 files lack `Amount Consumed`; some have `Unnamed: 0`, `RecordIndex`
  or `Sugar`; meal labels vary in case and numbering. METs, where missing, are derived from
  `Calories (Activity)` x 60 / kg, recorded in `static["activity_source"]`.
- The Shanghai zip contains a `__MACOSX` decoy folder that sorts first on Windows.
- Shanghai headers matched the loader's patterns on the first try, and the English diet text is one food
  per line ("Rice 150 g"), which makes the food table tractable (2,765 distinct strings).
