# Milestone 4 kickoff: decisions of 8 Oct 2026

Taken by the team lead at the start of Milestone 4, on Claude's recommendations. Nothing here changes a result.

## 1. The headline clause is reworded

"...and when to wear a sensor again" becomes **"...and what we measured about when the report stops being
true"**.

Why: what stands behind the old clause is the expiry record ("not a rule for when a patient should wear a
sensor") and a prompt that, in the product's own use case (an outpatient seen weeks after a fourteen-day
report, testing less often), is outside everything tested and so is shown on the Evidence screen only
(`2026-10-08-staleness.md`, "Consequences for the product"). The first screen could not deliver the old clause.
The new one says what the Evidence screen shows.

Changed in the same commit: README, CLAUDE.md, `docs/PROGRESS.md`, the design spec of 4 Oct. Earlier decision
records keep the wording they were written with.

## 2. The fingerstick estimate gets a band, scored once, before science stops

The estimate of section F (`chhaya.eval.fingersticks.estimates`: control, live, in hindsight) has no band, and
no coverage was ever scored for it. Rule 5 forbids drawing an estimate without its band, and the fingersticks
record says the running estimate is "shown with its band". The band Gate 2 scored exists only for CGMacros
participants, who have no fingersticks.

Decision: one descriptive pass (no bar), designed and registered in a dated note to Amendment 3 before its
code, run on development patients and then once on held-out Shanghai patients. It derives the band from
quantities fixed before the split and reports its coverage. The estimator of section F stays frozen.

Cut rule, written now: if the pass is not run by **10 Oct, 16:00**, it is dropped. Shanghai patients then show
measured things only (the wear's profile, fingersticks as read, the report figures, the prompt), and the
estimated trace and the reveal are drawn on CGMacros participants and on the synthetic demo patient only.

## 3. How the product is built

- **Front end:** plain ES modules and hand-built SVG charts, no runtime dependencies, served by one Python
  command. No Node on a clean clone. Streamlit stays the fallback named in the roadmap (decision time
  13 Oct, 18:00).
- **Patient data stays out of git (rule 6):** per-reading traces of real patients are patient data. The
  committed bundle holds the synthetic demo patient and the aggregates behind the Evidence screen; real
  held-out patients appear after `download` and a local build into a git-ignored folder.
- **Who is on screen:** every held-out patient of both cohorts, labelled by cohort, none left out. Where one
  patient is featured, the rule that picked them is stated on screen.
- **Wording:** every sentence of safety text on screen is a record's reviewed wording, and every result is its
  record's "claim to quote". The design carries the attraction; the claims stay as measured.
- **Type:** Source Sans 3, Source Serif 4 and Source Code Pro (SIL Open Font License), vendored. For the
  mocks they were copied from the copies Streamlit ships in the local environment and are not committed;
  the build adds them with the licence file.

## 4. Design direction (mocks)

`docs/superpowers/specs/m4-mocks/`: one page, three views, one token block, light and dark. Ink is what was
measured, indigo is what was estimated, a dashed edge marks an estimate or a simulation, and status colours
are kept for the evidence grades. All patient data in the mocks is synthetic; the Evidence figures are quoted
from the records. The mocks are a direction to approve, not the product.
