Governance Rule (Zero Redistribution)
Raw patient traces are never redistributed in the Git repository. They are fetched dynamically via automated checksum-verified download scripts (python -m chhaya.data.download) directly from authoritative archives to respect patient privacy and original license conditions. Per-reading time series remain local; only aggregate evaluation benchmarks and the synthetic patient are committed.

5. Front-End & Media Assets
Front-End, Audio & Synthetic Avatar Disclosure Native & MIT
  • Front-End Architecture: 100% Native Vanilla HTML5, Vanilla CSS3, and dynamic SVG rendering (src/chhaya/dashboard/static/app.js). Zero external JavaScript frameworks (no React, Vue, Angular, D3, or Chart.js) and zero telemetry or analytics scripts.
  • Ambient Audio (video/out/music.mp3): Royalty-free acoustic soundtrack utilized strictly for video demonstration and walkthrough mixing.
  • Demo Patient (Mrs. R.): Synthetic clinical avatar (58 yr female, HbA1c 7.8 %, Fasting glucose 142 mg/dL, Metformin 1000 mg bid, BMI 28.4 kg/m²) with zero real-world patient identity, licensed under MIT terms.
