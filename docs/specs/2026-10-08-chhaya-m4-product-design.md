# Chhaya, Milestone 4: the product (design)

Date: 8 Oct 2026. Follows `2026-10-04-chhaya-m3-design.md` ("Product (Milestone 4)") and supersedes that
section where they differ. Decisions behind it: `docs/decisions/2026-10-08-m4-kickoff.md`. Visual direction,
approved by the team lead on 8 Oct with three changes that are already in it (white page by default, clinical
colour on the sensor, pseudonyms): `docs/specs/m4-mocks/`.

## What it is

A single-page dashboard that runs offline from one command and reads only a pre-built bundle of JSON. Nothing
is fitted while it runs. Three screens, each with one decision, and a limits line that never leaves the screen.

| Screen | The one question | Biggest thing on it |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic list | Whose sensor report is old? | Days since the sensor, per patient |
| Patient | Does this report still describe this patient? | The shadow with its band, and the reveal |
| Evidence | Why should I believe any of it? | Every result beside its bar, misses included |

Who it is for: a diabetologist seeing a patient after a sensor report, and a judge deciding in two minutes
whether this is a product or a demo.

## What it never shows

No dose recommendation. No low-glucose estimate and nothing that reads as an alert on lows. No time in range
and no time below range from any estimate. No GMI or estimated HbA1c from fingersticks. The word "alarm"
nowhere on screen. No ranking of patients by risk, flag or prompt. No estimate without its band and the word
"estimated". No fusion "gain". A sensor low reads "sensor low, unconfirmed" and is drawn as a muted dotted
line, never in a warning colour.

## Architecture

```
results/**/summary.json ─┐
docs/decisions (wording) ─┼─> chhaya.build ──> bundle/ ──> chhaya.dashboard (static server) ──> browser
recordings + cached traces┘      (Python)       (JSON)        (stdlib http.server)             (ES modules)
```

New code, with no change to any existing module under `src/chhaya/`:

```
src/chhaya/
  product/
    claims.py      the nine results: grade, bars, claim to quote, command, record path, figure spec
    wording.py        every sentence of reviewed on-screen wording, as templates with named gaps
    names.py       pseudonym for a patient id (deterministic); honorific from the recorded sex
    record.py      Recording.static -> FHIR-shaped JSON
    synthetic.py   the recording of the synthetic demo patient "Mrs. R."
    treatment.py   "treatment changed since the sensor", from the dose table
    patient.py     one patient's bundle from a Recording and its estimates
    evidence.py    results/**/summary.json + claims.py -> evidence.json
  build.py         python -m chhaya.build [--real --confirm] [--out DIR]
  dashboard/
    __main__.py    python -m chhaya.dashboard [--bundle DIR] [--port N] [--no-open]
    static/        index.html, css/, js/, fonts/ (with OFL.txt)
    demo/          the committed bundle: index.json, evidence.json, patients/mrs-r.json
```

Data flows one way and the browser is the last step: the dashboard imports nothing from the model and the
model knows nothing of the dashboard. The contract between them is the bundle.

### The bundle

```
index.json                 build provenance; one row per patient for the clinic list
evidence.json              the Evidence screen: nine results, limits, data and licences
patients/<id>.json         everything the patient screen draws
```

- **Demo bundle** (`src/chhaya/dashboard/demo/`, committed): the synthetic patient and `evidence.json`. Built
  by `python -m chhaya.build` with no dataset on disk. A test rebuilds it and compares it with the committed
  files, so every number in it is regenerated by a command (rule 8).
- **Local bundle** (`artifacts/`, git-ignored): the demo bundle plus every held-out patient of both cohorts.
  Built by `python -m chhaya.build --real --confirm` after `download` and the trace cache. Per-reading
  arrays of real patients are patient data and never enter git (rule 6).
- The server uses `artifacts/` when it exists and the demo bundle otherwise, and the footer says which.

`patients/<id>.json`, in mg/dL and minutes of the day:

| Key | Holds |
|---|---|
| `id`, `name`, `cohort` | dataset identifier; display name; `synthetic`, `supervised` or `free-living` |
| `record` | the FHIR-shaped record |
| `wear` | length in days; mean, share above 180, share below 70; daily profile (5th, 25th, 50th, 75th, 95th percentile by time of day) |
| `days_since` | whole days from the split to the last day shown |
| `treatment` | `changed`, `none` or `not_recorded`, with the day when changed |
| `days[]` | per day since the sensor: times, estimate, band low and high, average day, the hidden sensor, meals, fingersticks as read, share of that day's readings inside the band |
| `since` | `shown`, `withheld` or `outside`, with the reason; fingerstick count and days; mean and share above 180, sensor-equivalent and as read; the times of day sampled |
| `prompt` | `raised`, `not_raised` or `not_computed`, with the day or the reason; the report's mean and the sensor-equivalent fingerstick mean beside it |
| `whatif` | for one logged meal: the estimate and its band at each offered carbohydrate amount; absent where not built |

### Who is on screen

- Every held-out patient of both cohorts that the committed runs scored, none left out, labelled by cohort.
  Development patients are not shown. Where one patient is featured (the default patient opened from a cold
  start is the synthetic one), the rule is stated on screen.
- **Names are pseudonyms.** `names.py` maps a patient identifier to a surname from a fixed list by a hash;
  the honorific follows the sex the dataset records and is left out where none is recorded. The dataset's
  identifier is shown beside the name, and the clinic list says: "Names are pseudonyms given by Chhaya for
  display. The datasets contain no names." The synthetic patient stays "Mrs. R.".
- Real recordings are shown in days since the sensor, not calendar dates.

### The synthetic patient is run through the real pipeline

`synthetic.py` makes a `Recording` by simulating the twin with known parameters (as the test fixtures do):
a five-day sensor wear, then six days with meals logged, six fingersticks a day, one unlogged snack, a
recorded change of treatment on day 2 and a downward drift after it. The build then treats her like any other
patient: `run_reveal` draws the shadow and its band, the fingerstick code computes the figures since the
sensor, and `chhaya.twin.staleness` decides the prompt at the threshold in `results/staleness/`. Whatever
those say is what the screen shows. The scenario was built to contain a drift, and the screen says the
patient and her sensor are simulated. Her genetic-marker field is synthetic and is read by nothing.

She sits inside the prompt's tested range on purpose, so that every panel has something to show. What the
product cannot do in its own use case (an outpatient weeks after a fourteen-day report) is shown by the real
patients whose prompt reads "not computed", and on the Evidence screen.

### Guards

- `--real` reads held-out patients, so it needs `--confirm`, committed code and the registered settings, as
  `chhaya.eval.traces` does. It scores nothing and writes nothing under `results/`.
- CGMacros traces come from the cache that `traces.py` already checks against the Gate 2 run.
- For Shanghai the build recomputes the frozen estimator and stops unless its prompt counts give back the
  committed ones (7 of 11 drifted, 2 of 21 stable at k = 3).
- A leakage test for the bundle: change the hidden sensor readings of a recording and assert that nothing in
  its bundle except the `sensor` arrays and the band share moves.
- `evidence.py` reads numbers only from `results/`; `claims.py` holds wording only. A test checks every
  claim against its decision record and every figure value against the claim that quotes it.
- `wording.py` is tested against the "Consequences for the product" sections, sentence by sentence.

## The three screens

Layout, hierarchy and components are as in the mocks. What each element is fed by:

**Always visible.** Header with the three screens, patient search (Ctrl K) and the theme toggle. Under it:
"Research prototype; evaluated on a Chinese cohort in supervised care and a US free-living cohort; estimates,
not measurements; not for dosing."

**Clinic list.** Columns: patient (name, cohort, identifier), sensor wear, days since the sensor, treatment
changed since the sensor (from the record), prompt to consider a new sensor wear, fingersticks a day. Sortable
by any column, listed by name by default, filterable by cohort. Three worded states with an icon in each state
column, never blank. Under the list, the two reviewed sentences and the line on pseudonyms.

**Patient.**
1. Identity, the record button, and the fact band: days since the sensor (the largest number on the screen,
   no threshold), sensor wear, fingersticks since, treatment, and the reviewed note on drift.
2. The hero: one day of the shadow with its 80 % band, the average day, logged meals and fingersticks as
   read; a readout strip that follows the cursor or the arrow keys; day selector; "Reveal the sensor" and a
   scrubber that opens the hidden trace from the left in 600 ms (at once under reduced motion); the share of
   the day's readings inside the band after the reveal; the reviewed sentence on the band; a table of the
   same values.
3. Since the sensor, from fingersticks: sensor-equivalent beside as-read, mean and share above 180, with the
   reviewed text; or "withheld" or "outside the tested range" with the reason.
4. Fingersticks as read, and the prompt in one of its three states at equal weight, with the plain comparison
   beside it and the reviewed text under it.
5. Last sensor report (measured): daily profile in the clinic's colours, mean, share above 180, share below 70
   marked "sensor low, unconfirmed".
6. Treatment changed since the sensor, with the reviewed case-series sentence.
7. Meal what-if, labelled simulation. 8. What keeps the report true. 9. The record.

**Evidence.** A tally (bars passed, bars missed, descriptive results), the one sentence on fusion, then the
nine results in a fixed order. Each: grade badge, the bars it had, the claim to quote in full, the bar as
written beforehand, cohort, the command that regenerates it, its record, and one small figure (dots with
intervals on a labelled scale, or the count itself). Then limits, data and licences.

### On-screen states that must exist

Loading (skeleton of the same shape, no layout shift), bundle missing (says which command builds it), a
patient with no fingersticks, a patient with no hidden sensor in the bundle (reveal control absent, with the
reason), estimate absent (Shanghai, if the band pass is cut: the hero shows the wear's profile and the
fingersticks as read, with "no estimated trace: its band was not scored"), what-if not built.

## Design system

One token block, defined once (`static/css/tokens.css`, taken from the mocks):

- **Colour.** White page; neutrals leaning toward indigo. Indigo is the only accent and means "estimated".
  The measured sensor is teal inside 70 to 180, amber above 180, muted and dotted below 70. Fingersticks are
  rose diamonds. A dashed edge means estimate or simulation; a solid edge means measured. Green, red and slate
  are kept for Passed, Missed and Descriptive, always with an icon and the word. The four line colours pass
  the colour-blind checks of the dataviz validator on both surfaces (all pairs), and are also told apart by
  mark type.
- **Type.** Source Serif 4 for titles and claims, Source Sans 3 for everything else and for all figures,
  Source Code Pro for commands. Vendored with the SIL Open Font License. Tabular figures in tables and axes.
- **Space, radius, elevation, motion.** 8-point steps; three radii; two shadows; 160 and 280 ms for interface
  changes and 600 ms for the reveal, all off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Themes.** Light for everyone by default; dark behind the toggle and remembered per browser.
- **Access.** WCAG AA contrast for text, visible focus rings, every control reachable by keyboard, the chart
  readable with the arrow keys and as a table, 16 px gutters and no sideways scroll at phone width.
- **Offline.** No network request leaves the machine: fonts and scripts are local files.

## The band of the fingerstick estimate

Decided 8 Oct; registered in a dated note to Amendment 3 before its code. The estimator of section F is
frozen. Two constructions of an 80 % band, both from quantities fixed before the split, are compared on
development patients; one is frozen; its coverage is reported once on held-out patients, on average and per
patient, descriptive, no bar. If it is run by 10 Oct, 16:00, Shanghai patients get the estimated trace with
that band and that sentence. If not, they show measured things only.

## Verification

- Python: tests first for every module under `product/`, `build.py` and the server (status codes, no path
  outside the bundle and static folders is served).
- The page: run it, drive it in the browser at desktop, tablet and phone width in both themes, read the
  console, and compare each number on the Evidence screen with `results/`.
- Reviews: a code review on the Python, the clinical-wording review on every screen's wording before the
  freeze, an independent code review on the band pass and on the bundle's leakage test.

## Cut order

Cut first, in this order: the meal what-if; patient search; polish of the dark theme. Then what-if for real
patients, the table view of the small charts. Never cut: the reveal, the band, the limits line, the Evidence
screen, the three states of the prompt. Streamlit replaces the custom page only if the patient screen does
not work end to end on 13 Oct at 18:00.

## Out of scope

Accounts, saving anything, live fitting, uploads, notifications, any second language, print styles, and a
hosted copy. The recorded walkthrough and the README screenshots belong to Milestone 5 and use this page.
