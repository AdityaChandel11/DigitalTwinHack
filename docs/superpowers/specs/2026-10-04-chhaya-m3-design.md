# Chhaya from Milestone 3 on: design

Date: 4 Oct 2026. Supersedes the Milestone 3 and 4 sections of the roadmap as written on 2 Oct. Reasons are in
`docs/decisions/2026-10-04-plan-revision.md`; pass bars are in Amendment 3 of `docs/PREREGISTRATION.md`.

## What we are building, in one sentence

Chhaya turns one sensor wear into the patient's shadow: it tells the doctor **how long that sensor report stays
true, which of its readings not to believe, what keeps it true, and when to wear a sensor again**, with an
honest band. (Revised 8 Oct: the clause "and it predicts post-meal excursions without the sensor" is removed;
Gate 3 did not support it. See `docs/decisions/2026-10-08-gate3.md`.)

## Who it is for, and the one decision

A diabetologist seeing a patient weeks after a 14-day sensor report. The decision on the screen:

> Is the last sensor report still valid? Where in the day is the problem (fasting, after which meal)? Should
> this patient wear a sensor again?

The dashboard simulates options the doctor enters. It never recommends a dose.

## What counts as success

1. Every number shown is regenerated by one command and was scored once on held-out patients against a bar
   written beforehand. Results that miss their bar are shown with the bar.
2. The brief's three requirements are met with measured evidence: two streams fused (and the fusion ablated),
   an adverse event predicted ahead of time, a doctor-facing dashboard.
3. A reader of the README's first screen sees the reveal, four honest numbers and what Chhaya is not.

## What "digital twin" means here (and nothing more)

| Part | What it is | Evidence it rests on |
|---|---|---|
| Personal profile | The patient's daily glucose shape from one sensor wear, with a band | Gate 2; Shanghai fingerstick experiment |
| Physiology | The E-DES model with seven personal parameters; the record sets its prior, the sensor is the evidence | Gate 2 (adds about 2 % for five days when meals are logged) |
| Upkeep | Fingersticks nudge the estimate and feed the staleness alarm | Experiment F and the staleness test |
| Foresight | Withdrawn 8 Oct. Experiment M (post-meal excursion at meal time, sensor off) missed M1, M2 and M3; the dashboard shows the measured arm accuracies and risk bands only | Gate 3 record |
| What-if | Swap or resize a meal on the virtual patient; labelled simulation, associational | Per-meal predicted against observed rise (r = 0.42 pooled) |

Cut because we cannot defend them: low-glucose alarms, dose or drug what-ifs, walk what-ifs, time-in-range
estimation, any claim to replace a sensor.

### Rules every experiment module follows

- Two commands per experiment: a development run (default; cross-validated on development patients, never
  loads a test patient into a model or a score) and a confirmatory run (`--confirm`; fits on development
  patients, scores test patients once, writes `summary.json`, `report.md`, `provenance.json`).
- A leakage test for every feature builder: change the hidden sensor readings and assert that every feature
  outside the sensor-on arm is unchanged.
- Results folders hold aggregates only (rule 6 in CLAUDE.md): no per-meal rows, no per-patient lab values.
- Sensor lows are never a training label. A sensor low on screen reads "sensor low, unconfirmed".

## Product (Milestone 4)

Three screens, read from pre-computed artifacts; nothing is fitted live.

1. **Clinic list.** Patients ordered by how stale their twin is and by share of meals predicted above 180, with
   a one-line reason.
2. **Patient.** The last sensor report beside today's shadow with its band; the reveal switch; days since the
   sensor and the staleness state; a strip showing where in the day the excursions are; a stream switch (record
   only, sensor history only, fingersticks only, fused) that changes the excursion probability **and shows the
   measured accuracy of that arm next to it**; a meal what-if labelled simulation.
3. **Evidence.** Every results table with its bar, the label check, limits, data provenance.

A limits banner is always visible: research prototype; evaluated on a Chinese cohort in supervised care and a US free-living cohort;
estimates, not measurements; not for dosing.

Front end: a single page served by one local command, with Streamlit as the fallback if the custom page is not
working by 13 Oct. No Docker. A recorded walkthrough ships with the repo.

The record is FHIR-shaped JSON. The demo patient "Mrs. R." is synthetic and labelled so.

## Known limits we will state

No Indian data. Shanghai is supervised care with treatment being adjusted. Hidden windows are at most eleven
days, and these data do not give a number of days for how long a report stays true: about 1 mg/dL per day,
downward, in supervised care; none detected over a week in free-living participants; eight patients re-recorded
up to 168 days later (a case series, not a rule). The effect of the physiology is about 2 %. The
shadow is about one and a half times as far from a fingerstick as a real sensor is. No exogenous insulin, no
drug kinetics, no counter-regulation in the model.
